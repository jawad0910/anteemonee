Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as:

  • Account information (name, email, phone number)
  • Study details (province, board, class)
  • Usage data (study time, practice attempts)
  • Anonymous device identifier (used only to keep one account active on a single device)
  • Content you create (notes, flashcards, chat messages)

The Century Club page is public. It lists the first 100 accounts by join date and shows only a display name, username, profile photo, join date and position on the list. Your email address, phone number and study details are never published there.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Anteemonee platform
  • Personalize your learning experience
  • Track academic progress and provide recommendations
  • Communicate with you about updates and features
  • Ensure platform security and prevent abuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

4. AI Memory

When AI Memory is enabled, Anteemonee remembers useful facts from your AI chats (such as your name, goals, weak or strong topics, language preference, and exam targets) so the tutor can personalize future answers. This memory:

  • Is stored privately on your account and is never shared with other users
  • Is used only to personalize your own AI responses
  • Never includes passwords, OTPs, payment details, or other credentials. Our system is instructed to ignore such information
  • Is on by default, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings, then AI, then AI Memory. Turning it off stops new learning and stops the AI from using stored memories
  • Can be permanently deleted anytime with the Clear AI Memory button in Settings, and is deleted automatically if you delete your account

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption, access controls, and regular security audits.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Anteemonee app or at our support channels.
